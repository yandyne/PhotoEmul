PhotoEmul is a native macOS editor built on the spektrafilm spectral film engine. PhotoEmul itself is free software under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (LICENSE.txt). Film and paper profiles, lens data and other third-party material it ships with are listed below with their licenses.

PhotoEmul은 spektrafilm 스펙트럼 필름 엔진 위에 만든 macOS 편집기입니다. 아래는 이 앱이 사용하는 모든 오픈소스 소프트웨어와 데이터, 그리고 각 라이선스입니다.

Engines

spektrafilm OFX — Aedan Diez (github.com/chaert-s, spektrafilm.114c.de). The GPU rendering core (SpektraFilmAppCore, SpektraFilm.metallib) that PhotoEmul links is built from the spektrafilm OFX source repository, licensed under GPL-3.0. PhotoEmul is a user-built derivative of that GPL source, not an official OFX binary distribution; the official-binary notice and the exported-LUT license of the OFX project do not apply to it.

spektrafilm (reference implementation) — Andrea Volpato, github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm, GPL-3.0. The density-emulation model, the negative → print → scan pipeline and the profile-generation foundation come from this work. Spectral film modeling powered by spektrafilm. Please cite the repository / Zenodo DOI (CITATION.cff) in academic work.

Spectral colour science

Hanatos 2025 spectral upsampling — Johannes Hanika, vkdt (github.com/hanatos/vkdt), GPL-3.0. SpektraHanatos2025Spectra.f32 bundled with this app is the spectral upsampling table in the Hanatos 2025 model direction.

Mallett & Yuksel 2019, “Spectral Primary Decomposition for Rendering with sRGB Reflectance”, EGSR 2019 (diglib.eg.org). The alternate spectral reconstruction path follows this method.

CIE standard observer and illuminants — CIE 1931 colour-matching functions, illuminant A and the D series are public CIE definitions. Scene light spectra are analytic models written for PhotoEmul (Planck's law at CIE colour temperatures, Gaussian emission bands for fluorescent and sodium lamps); no measured lamp tables are included.

Build-time table generation used colour-science (BSD-3-Clause, colour-science.org), NumPy (BSD-3-Clause) and SciPy (BSD-3-Clause). None of these libraries ship inside the app; only the tables they produced do.

Film, paper and filter data

spektrafilm profiles — Andrea Volpato and contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0 (SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt in the spektrafilm repository). The JSON film, paper, enlarger-filter and LUT data under the engine's data folder are these profiles. Profiles that PhotoEmul derived from them (Ilford, T-Max, Eterna, instant films, the developer variants and others) carry a “derivation” note describing the modification, as CC BY-SA requires, and are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Characteristic curves, spectral sensitivities and dye densities in those profiles were digitised from the manufacturers' public technical datasheets (Kodak, Fujifilm, Ilford / Harman, Polaroid, CineStill).

Reciprocity failure fits (exposure correction and colour shift on long exposures) follow the manufacturers' public datasheets; the fits are PhotoEmul's own.

Lens profiles

lensfun database (lensfun.github.io) — distortion, lateral chromatic aberration and vignetting profiles, converted by PhotoEmul's own parser into LensProfiles.json. The lensfun database is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0); the converted LensProfiles.json in this app is shared under the same license.

Lightroom Classic plug-in

The optional PhotoEmul.lrplugin is written against the Adobe Lightroom Classic SDK (Lua). The plug-in itself is GPL-3.0 like the app. It hands photos to PhotoEmul.app for editing and bundles no third-party code.
